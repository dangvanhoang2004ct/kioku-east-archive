Condition Guide

A clear checklist for documenting one specific figure. KIOKU has not inspected any items shown in this reference catalogue.

Make a condition note
What to document

One model. Different histories.

Two copies of the same figure may look alike in a manufacturer catalogue but differ as physical items. A careful record describes the object in hand rather than relying on a model name or a polished reference image.

01 · Figure

Surface and paint

Check the front, back, base, joints, and finish under even light. Photograph marks and repairs clearly.

02 · Box

Packaging condition

Record whether the original box is present. Note creases, tears, fading, and opened seals separately.

03 · Parts

Accessories and base

Compare included pieces against an identified official contents list. Mark unknown or missing parts plainly.

04 · Defects

Observed flaws

Describe what is visible and where. Do not turn an unexamined area into a claim of perfect condition.

Use dated photos of the exact item. A catalogue reference helps identify a line; it cannot establish the condition or authenticity of a physical figure.
