About KIOKU

Kioku means memory or recollection in Japanese. The passage of time is the brand metaphor for the way familiar stories stay with us.

A collector's reference archive

Time flows. Memories remain.

Anime can bring us worlds that travel with us long after the screen goes dark. The figures we choose to keep can become quiet reminders of who we were when those stories first mattered.

KIOKU is an independent figure-reference project focused on Japanese maker and figure lines, including Bandai Spirits and MegaHouse. The archive is designed to make discovery simple and to keep reference information distinct from the condition and details of an actual item.

Clear references, honest records.

Catalogue entries help identify a maker and line; they do not prove that a specific item exists, is authentic, or is available for sale. Any future listing would need exact-item photographs, independently recorded figure and box condition, included parts, price, and verified fulfillment terms.

KIOKU is not presented as an authorized distributor or affiliated with the makers named in the catalogue. Their official pages are linked as references where useful.
