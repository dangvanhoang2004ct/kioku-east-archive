A COLLECTOR REFERENCE

Condition guide

A practical checklist for documenting a specific pre-owned figure. Kioku has not inspected any items in this preview.

Make a condition note
What to document

One model. Different histories.

Two copies of the same figure may look alike in a manufacturer catalogue but differ dramatically as physical items. Future Kioku listings are designed to document the exact object, not only its model name.

Figure

Surface & paint

Photographed views, paint wear, scratches, joints, discoloration and observed repairs.

Box

Packaging

Box present or absent, opened or sealed if verified, and visible packaging wear.

Parts

Included pieces

Base, accessories, removable parts, and what is missing from the exact item.

Defects

Visible flaws

Scratches, paint wear, discoloration, repairs and other observed defects, shown in item-specific photographs.

No box? No assumptions.

A figure without its original box can still be meaningful to a collector. Its packaging status should never be hidden inside a generic condition grade, and its completeness must be checked separately.

Manufacturer reference images are not substitutes for photographs of the physical figure that a buyer would receive. This page is a proposed policy framework, not an operational inspection certificate.

Clarity before checkout.

For actual transactions, Kioku must show confirmed price, stock, shipping origin, taxes/duties information and applicable terms. Those systems are not activated in this design preview.
